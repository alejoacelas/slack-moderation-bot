# Bot avatar

The app icon is Irasutoya's [門番のイラスト](https://www.irasutoya.com/2018/08/blog-post_760.html)
(a Sengoku-era gatekeeper), cropped to the upper body on a pastel yellow
background with a coral "ゴゴ" in the corner. It isn't stored here because
Irasutoya's [terms](https://www.irasutoya.com/p/terms.html) don't allow
redistributing its illustrations. To rebuild it, download the `s800` image from
that page and crop it to a 1024px square.
